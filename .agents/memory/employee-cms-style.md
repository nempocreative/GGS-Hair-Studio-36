---
name: Employee CMS style
description: Preferred copy and typography for GGS employee management screens in the CMS.
---

“Employee CMS” refers to the signed-in employee workspace at `/employee/...` in the visitor app, not the owner-only employee-account manager in the admin app.

Keep the employee workspace concise, professional, and practical. Use standard system typography such as Arial or Poppins; avoid marketing-style hero copy, generic AI aesthetics, filler text, padded counts, decorative metric icons, and repeated labels.

On iPad and tablet portrait, use the same responsive presentation as on a phone, including the visitor bottom tab bar. Keep employee navigation in its sidebar/drawer rather than moving it into the bottom bar.

**Why:** The user clarified the intended screen with a screenshot after an earlier change targeted the wrong employee page, and later asked for the cashier screen to look professional and attractive without AI-like styling or unnecessary text. They also explicitly asked for portrait tablets to match the phone layout while preserving employee menu placement.

**How to apply:** When the user asks to refine the employee CMS, start with the signed-in employee workspace unless they explicitly refer to owner account administration. For responsive work, check portrait tablet and phone layouts together; only the visitor tab bar belongs at the bottom.
