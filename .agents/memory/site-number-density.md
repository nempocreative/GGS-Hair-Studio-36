---
name: Site number density
description: Keep public GGS pages from feeling cluttered with decorative numbers.
---

Avoid decorative serial numbers, unnecessary item counts, and sample voucher codes on public GGS pages. Keep numbers that communicate useful information, such as prices, discounts, and service durations.

**Why:** The user asked that the website not have too many numbers; retaining meaningful offer and service details keeps the pages useful while reducing clutter.

**How to apply:** When adding public-facing cards, lists, and carousels, do not add visible numeric indexes or counters unless they help the visitor make a decision.
