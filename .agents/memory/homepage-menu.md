For the GGS visitor website, show four menu shortcuts per row and eight per slide. Keep remaining shortcuts on the next slide, use dot navigation without arrow buttons, and show all shortcuts without white or boxed backgrounds. In particular, do not add separate boxed styling to Men's Grooming, Women's Hair & Beauty, or Hair & Self Care. On the homepage, place the voucher cards directly after the menu without a visible heading, link, or disclaimer between them.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified these requirements after prior menu changes did not match their intent.

**How to apply:** Preserve this layout, overflow behavior, navigation control, and background-free styling when adding or changing homepage shortcuts.
