name: GGS responsive coverage
description: The user's project-wide expectation for responsive behavior across GGS website and management areas.
---

For GGS, responsive work applies to every visitor page, the My GGS account area, employee workspace, and owner CMS, across phone widths and iPad portrait and landscape.

On the visitor website, show multiple compact cards side by side on phones and iPads instead of one oversized card at a time.

On the homepage, show all three “Temui orang di balik gaya” profile cards in one row on iPad; keep the phone layout at two columns.

On iPad, show three cards at a time in the men's and women's service carousels and three hairstyle model cards per row.

**Why:** The user explicitly requests multi-card layouts across the visitor website, three cards per page in the service and model sections, and a single-row team showcase on iPad.

**How to apply:** Check representative routes from each area at small, typical, and large phone widths plus iPad portrait and landscape. Keep visitor-site card collections compact and multi-column while preserving readable text and usable controls. On iPad, keep the homepage team cards in one row and show three service/model cards at a time; preserve the phone layouts and visitor/employee bottom navigation. Do not bypass authentication to inspect protected screens; state clearly when a signed-in view could not be verified.
