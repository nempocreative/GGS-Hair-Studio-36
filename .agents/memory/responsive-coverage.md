For GGS, responsive work applies to every visitor page, the My GGS account area, employee workspace, and owner CMS, across phone widths and iPad portrait and landscape.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested precise layouts across all pages on iPad and phone screen sizes.

**How to apply:** Check representative routes from each area at small, typical, and large phone widths plus iPad portrait and landscape. Preserve the visitor and employee bottom navigation behavior. Do not bypass authentication to inspect protected screens; state clearly when a signed-in view could not be verified.
