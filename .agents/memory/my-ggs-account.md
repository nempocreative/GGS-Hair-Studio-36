Treat the Pengguna area in My GGS as a marketplace-style customer account home: include points, access to buying products, and other useful account information. Keep text concise; the user said the earlier profile had too much text.

**Why:** The user described this as the intended contents of the Pengguna account and asked to reduce excess text.

**How to apply:** Keep the customer dashboard compact and centered on account tasks, points, product shopping, and useful information. Preserve clear distinction between demo and real account data.

Customers may choose either email or WhatsApp as their account identifier and set their own password. Do not add Google sign-in or OTP verification.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose email-or-WhatsApp/password accounts without Google or OTP; without verification, automated account recovery cannot safely prove ownership.

**How to apply:** Keep the identifier choice clear on sign-up and sign-in, and do not promise verified contact details or automated password recovery without a separately approved verification method.
