name: Public site copy
description: Copy tone and CMS-data integrity for the GGS visitor website.
---

Use plain, specific Indonesian for visitor-facing copy. Preserve any tagline or introduction customized in the CMS; only replace the legacy stock wording with a natural display fallback. Do not invent GGS location, hours, phone, or social details when the CMS fields are empty.

**Why:** The site had generic promotional copy and empty real-world contact details; making up missing studio facts would mislead visitors.

**How to apply:** When revising GGS public-site copy or fallback values, keep custom CMS text working and require verified owner-provided values for address, hours, and contact information.
